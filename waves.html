Notes as waves · a look

19 Nov 2026 · a look in the lab, nothing of the engine changed · work/maqam/lab/waves.py · made 19 Nov 2026 05:23 · the iterations

«the notes are frequencies, the frequencies are waves. We treat them as separated ones next to each other… what if we will merge them, look for their inner product or perhaps other calculation that will show relations between them and how they affect each other?» · «there is a multi dimensional presentation to those maqams»

What was computed

The notes of a stretch are merged into one thing and laid on waves that go round the octave: wave 1 once, wave 2 twice, up to wave 24. For each wave, the inner product of the stretch's held notes (each weighing its seconds) with that wave gives two numbers: a size and an angle. So a stretch of music becomes a point with 24 sizes and 24 angles. Nothing else goes in: no grid of notes, no step sizes, no table of ajnas, no threshold.

The sizes do not change when the same music is played from another note. The angles say where it stands. Wave 12 is large when the notes sit a half tone apart; wave 7 is large when the octave is walked in seven near-even steps, which is what three-quarter tones do.
